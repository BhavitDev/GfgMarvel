THE FLAGSHIP CHAMPIONSHIP

Organized by the GeeksForGeeks Student Chapter at Bennett University, The Multiverse brings together the brightest coders, designers, and innovators.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions answered by the organizing team.

Who is eligible to participate?

Any undergraduate, postgraduate, or school student from any recognized institution in India is welcome to participate. Cross-college teams are completely allowed.

Is there any entry or registration fee?

No, registration is 100% free of charge! All meals, snacks, high-speed WiFi, mentorship, and event swags are provided completely free by the GFG Student Chapter.

What should I bring to the venue?

Please bring your laptop, charger/extension board, student ID card, and your digital Hero Access Pass generated during online registration.

What is the allowed team size?

Teams can consist of 1 to 4 members. You can participate solo or as part of a squad.

Will all participants receive certificates?

Yes! All verified attendees who submit their project or participate in coding duels will receive official digital Certificates of Participation co-branded by GeeksForGeeks and Bennett University.

How are projects evaluated?

Projects are evaluated by industry judges based on Technical Innovation (35%), Practical Usability (25%), UI/UX Design (20%), and Stage Presentation (20%).
